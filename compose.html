CLIMATE · ANTALYA, TURKEY

COP31 COLLAPSES: NO DEAL AS EU REJECTS PACT WITHOUT FOSSIL FUELS

The European Union formally rejected the draft Antalya Pact on Sunday, saying it “does not even mention fossil fuels”. Panama, Norway and island states joined the rejection. The summit, two days past its Friday deadline, ended with no agreement — the first COP to fail outright since Copenhagen in 2009. Nearly 200 nations deadlocked over a 130-country demand to keep the COP28 promise to move away from coal, oil and gas.

CONFIRMED Reuters · The Guardian · BBC — COP31 presidency, Antalya
WAR · THE GULF

GULF IN FLAMES: IRAN HITS U.S. BASES IN BAHRAIN; TWO CIVILIANS HURT

Bahrain said on Sunday that two people were hurt — one in critical condition — when an Iranian strike hit a civilian industrial facility on Saturday. Iran’s Revolutionary Guards claimed a combined missile-and-drone operation against U.S. positions at the al-Riffa and Awali bases, saying it destroyed two Patriot radars; the claim is unverified. Tehran also said it shot down a fourth U.S. MQ-1 drone in 24 hours east of the Strait of Hormuz.

CONFIRMED Reuters · AFP · Al Jazeera — Bahrain Interior Ministry; IRGC claim REPORTED
WAR · UKRAINE

DYMER FLATTENED: DRONE COLLAPSES FLATS, 14 MISSING UNDER RUBBLE

A Russian drone destroyed a five-storey apartment block in Dymer, north of Kyiv, in the early hours of Sunday. Rescuers pulled nine people out alive; President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said 14 people, including two children, were still unaccounted for, and two bodies were later recovered. Ukraine’s air force said Russia launched 186 drones overnight — 72 jet-powered — and 161 were downed or jammed, with hits at 18 sites.

CONFIRMED AP · Reuters · Ukraine SES — Zelenskyy; Kyiv regional administration

BALTIC FREIGHTER STILL BURNING AFTER DRONE HIT

Fire aboard the Russian cargo ship Sparta II burned through the night off Finland, a day after drones struck it in the Gulf of Finland. All 16 crew were evacuated. Finland says Ukraine was likely behind it. CONFIRMED Reuters

02

JAVA FACTORY FIRE: 24 BODY BAGS, DOZENS MISSING

Police removed 24 body bags from the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia candy factory in Cikande on Sunday, a day after fire tore through the plant. About 300 were on shift; 29 still missing. Search resumes Monday. CONFIRMED Reuters

03

SEOUL MINISTER QUITS OVER AI EXAM-CHEATING CHAOS

Education Minister Choi Kyo-jin resigned on Sunday; President Lee accepted within the hour, after last-minute anti-AI rules sowed confusion before the Suneung. 111 suspected cheating cases among 500,000 candidates. CONFIRMED Yonhap · AFP

04

“EL CHAPO ISIDRO” CAPTURED IN SINALOA RAID; 11 DEAD

Mexican special forces captured Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, an FBI Ten Most Wanted fugitive with a $5m reward, in Guasave on Friday night. Seven gunmen and four soldiers died; eight arrested. CONFIRMED AP · Reuters

05

MOSCOW CRIES “IRON CURTAIN” AS ESTONIA SEALS BORDER

Estonia will shut all road crossings with Russia from 1 December for at least six months, after an alleged GRU-ordered arson attempt on drone maker Threod Systems. Moscow vowed retaliation. CONFIRMED Al Jazeera · Reuters

06

BITCOIN CLAWS BACK $60,000 AFTER TWO-YEAR LOW

Bitcoin reclaimed $60,000 on Sunday, up 9% from a $55,130 low — weakest since September 2024 — after a $1.8bn liquidation wave eased. Abraxas Capital alone withdrew 17,597 BTC. REPORTED Reuters · CoinDesk

07

NIGERIA BOAT TOLL RISES TO 24 IN BAYELSA

The death toll from a boat capsize in Bayelsa state rose to 24 on Sunday, officials said, as NEMA sent a technical team to the scene. CONFIRMED Citizen Digital · Vanguard

08

ETHIOPIA LAUNCHES POLIO DRIVE FOR 11.3M CHILDREN

Ethiopia, with WHO, UNICEF and polio partners, launched a house-to-house vaccination campaign on Sunday aiming to protect more than 11.3 million children. OFFICIAL WHO · PANA

09

MONGOLIA HEADS FOR SNAP PRESIDENTIAL VOTE

Mongolia will hold a snap presidential election after President Khurelsukh resigned amid $25m bribery allegations; parliament approved it 96–8. Speaker Sandag holds presidential powers. CONFIRMED MONTSAME · AFP

10

BLACK FOILS WIN DUBAI SAILGP AS GBR TITLE HOPES DIE

New Zealand’s Black Foils won the Dubai SailGP on Sunday, beating Australia in the final race. Defending champions Emirates GBR missed the final, ending their title defence. CONFIRMED SailGP · Reuters
